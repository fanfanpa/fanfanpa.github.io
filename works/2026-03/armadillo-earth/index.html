Armadillo Earth

Two Armadillo projections — the same equal-area projection, one centred at longitude 180° and one at −180° — joined along the antimeridian into a single egg-shaped world map. Black landmasses on an ivory sphere, with a gray graticule, rotating continuously; the map is projected from scratch on every frame, and each of the two panels keeps its own thin black frame.
