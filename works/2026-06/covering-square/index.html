Covering a Square with Three Discs: A Gradient Descent Animation

Three translucent discs — orange, blue and purple — sliding into place over a square, driven by a gradient descent on a soft-coverage objective. Faint trails follow each disc’s centre, and a line of text above the plot reports the iteration, the soft coverage, the hard coverage and the best hard coverage found so far. The discs start from a random position on every load and settle at about 99% hard coverage.
