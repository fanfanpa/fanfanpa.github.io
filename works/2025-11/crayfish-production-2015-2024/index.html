China’s Crayfish Production, 2015–2024

A looping animation of crayfish production in China, running through the years 2015 to 2024 and then starting over. A horizontal bar chart ranks the provinces for the current year, led by Hubei with 143.7 by 2024; a smaller line chart laid over its lower right corner traces the national total climbing from about 70 to 344.8, with a red dashed line marking the year being shown.
